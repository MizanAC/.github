# Getting help

- Setting up Mizan on your server: ask in [Discord](https://discord.gg/fRbk43tKcV) or email contact@mizan.ac.
- A player you think was flagged wrongly: open the case on your community's dashboard, or email contact@mizan.ac. Don't
  post SteamIDs or player details in a public issue.
- A ban on a public ban list: see [disputing a ban](https://mizan.ac/disputes).
- A mistake in this repository's documentation: open an issue.
